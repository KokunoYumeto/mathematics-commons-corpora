# Rights boundary

The CC0 dedication in `LICENSE` covers only Commons-originated control metadata,
schemas, and documentation committed to this repository.

It does **not** relicense any upstream source, book, article, theorem text,
figure, media asset, formal declaration, translation, trademark, or name.
Every third-party component retains its own rights and attribution requirements.
Registry fields are evidence and routing controls, not legal advice.

Public payload is fail-closed. A programme or component whose redistribution
state is `rights_hold`, `unresolved`, or `component_audit_required` may publish
only non-infringing metadata until a versioned rights record permits more.

All independently produced editions must visibly state:

> Independently maintained, AI-produced edition. No upstream endorsement is
> claimed or implied.
